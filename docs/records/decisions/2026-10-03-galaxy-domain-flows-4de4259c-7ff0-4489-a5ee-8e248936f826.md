## 2026-10-03 — Galaxy counts how domains depend on each other

**Why**: Neural leaves the picker and its domain coupling needs a home; counted aggregates read better than bundles (McGee and Dingliana 2012); at the earlier G1 width the spike's 99 strands at 10k buried the galaxies.
**Prior**: replaces the unlanded G1 draft; narrows `.claude/rules/design.md`'s "no default edge mesh" to admit counted domain strands; 2026-09-06's rejection of bundling stands; one roll-up with the hex canals (#2259).
**Decision**: at the overview, gone between zoom ratio 1.4 and 2.2, Galaxy draws one strand per pair of domains whose capabilities declare dependencies across them: width min(2.4, 0.5 + 0.35·log2(1 + count)) px, tapered toward the depended-on domain or headed both ways, bent round other galaxies and the core, an opaque tone; the five strongest show their count, and so does every strand of the hovered or focused galaxy, while the rest recede.
**Dissent**: no strand ever names the capabilities behind its count, and selecting a concept draws only its own relations; the canvas count is a bare number, its unit only in the screen-reader list; the receded tone is below 3:1 by design.
**Falsifier**: asked which two domains depend on each other most, readers answer slower or worse on Galaxy than they did on Neural.
