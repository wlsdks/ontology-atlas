Each figure was an animation a visitor had to watch to the end to learn one fact, and together they were about 3,200 lines of source over two animation engines.
**Prior**: Overturns 2026-10-02 "The download page tells the product in one illustration under the hero" (f3f2b004) and the figure half of 2026-10-15 "The conduction figure waits until nearly whole in view" (acd0d175). 2026-10-03 (36af7b02) stands: nothing draws out of view; its "one figure plays at a time" has no subject left.
