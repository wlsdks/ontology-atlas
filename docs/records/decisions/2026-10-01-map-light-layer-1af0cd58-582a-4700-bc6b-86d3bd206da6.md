## 2026-10-01 — The map draws light on one WebGL2 layer, and the layer draws nothing else
**Why**: Inferred, not measured: the relation glow and the path signal need a continuous light along each curve that adds up where relations meet, which canvas `shadowBlur` or sprites would not carry at 10k emitters; AGENTS.md asks for a decision before another renderer.
**Prior**: `docs/records/decisions/2026-10-01-atlas-motion-identity-one-spring-one-light-d353955a-5f51-4315-a933-7a00e3af114f.md` (one light) stands; 2026-09-06 (no three.js on the map) stands: the layer is raw WebGL2.
**Decision**: `src/widgets/ontology-map/light/` owns one WebGL2 canvas beside the map that draws light only (a Gaussian strip per lit relation, radial blooms), `plus-lighter` while visible (`screen` where unsupported), created in an idle callback, in Flat and Galaxy, for an event only, absent under reduced motion and on software renderers. Every light runs in its relation's direction: directional source to target, symmetric from the attended end. A path hop the walk crosses backwards keeps its place in the sequence and lights source to target (owner decision, delegated). A path lights on the frame it appears, unless a concept is selected or the trail lens is on.
**Dissent**: A second canvas costs frames while a light lives: at synth 10k Flat it loses 10 of 220 frames per 2.4 s at DPR 1 (at DPR 2 it gains 6, 213 vs 207, as the comets it stands down cost more there); a keyboard focus keeps the sample map awake 3.2 s instead of 1.5 s; the context keeps 26 MB of GPU memory after its first light (1448x900). A light also waits for the reveal's ink instead of the plan's 180-420ms clamp.
**Falsifier**: At synth 10k Flat, DPR 2, headed Chromium, the light loses more than 12 frames per 2.4 s after a focus against the same build with its chunk blocked (median of three interleaved pairs).
