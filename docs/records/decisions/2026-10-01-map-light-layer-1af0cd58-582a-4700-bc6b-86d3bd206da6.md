## 2026-10-01 — The map draws light on one WebGL2 layer, and the layer draws nothing else
**Why**: The relation glow and the path signal need a continuous light along a curve that adds up where relations meet; canvas `shadowBlur` or sprites cost 10ms a frame at 10k emitters and `shadowBlur` is slow in WebKit, and AGENTS.md asks for a decision before another renderer.
**Prior**: `docs/records/decisions/2026-10-01-atlas-motion-identity-one-spring-one-light-d353955a-5f51-4315-a933-7a00e3af114f.md` (one light) stands; 2026-09-06 (three.js declined for the map) and the 2026-09-08 hero falsifier stand: the layer is raw WebGL2 and imports no scene.
**Decision**: `src/widgets/ontology-map/light/` owns one WebGL2 canvas beside the map canvas that draws light only: a Gaussian strip per lit relation and radial blooms, never a concept, label or line. It composites `plus-lighter` (`screen` where unsupported), is created in an idle callback, keeps one context per mounted map, runs in Flat and Galaxy for an event only (an idle map draws 0 frames), has no context under reduced motion, and stays off on a software renderer unless `?light=force`. Focus: up to 48 relations in the reveal's direction, riding the reveal front, 1100 px/s within 180-420ms, each reached neighbour blooms (tau 0.28s). Path: hop by hop within 1.2s. Comets and the galaxy glint stand down on a lit line.
**Dissent**: The plan asked every light to arrive by its 180-420ms clamp; on a cold focus that would run the light ahead of the reveal's ink, so a light waits for the ink and arrives at 0.33-0.45s (measured headed on the sample).
**Falsifier**: A recording where a light leaves its line during a camera dive, a map at rest that draws frames, a frame-budget miss that blocking the light chunk removes, or a WKWebView recording where `plus-lighter` flattens the layer.
