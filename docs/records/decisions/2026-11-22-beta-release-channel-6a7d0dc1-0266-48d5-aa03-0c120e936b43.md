## 2026-11-22 — A release may go out first as vX.Y.Z-beta.N, and the same bytes become vX.Y.Z

**Why**: owner, 2026-11-22. Ten releases in six days with two same-day chains of three patches: the falsifier of 2026-09-27 713780f2, a fix within 48 hours, fired.
**Prior**: overturns 2026-09-27 713780f2 ("every release is a plain vX.Y.Z tag", "no flag re-enables one"); keeps its staged draft and `release` approval, 2026-10-03 d7a8ee52 (Apple Silicon only) and 2026-08-01 "Ship Windows x64 as a public unsigned beta", whose installer rides both channels.
**Decision**: section 4 of `docs/specs/2026-11-22-ci-split-and-beta.md`. A beta is a GitHub pre-release with no `latest.json`; `promote-release.yml` republishes its checked assets as `vX.Y.Z` and builds, signs and tags nothing. A stable release publishes only over a whole green `e2e.yml` run on its commit; promotion has no waiting period, and a direct `vX.Y.Z` cut needs no beta.
**Dissent**: one person using a build for a day finds what one person finds; two betas of a version look alike in the app, which says `X.Y.Z` for both; a day per release against a cadence of two a day; a fix needs a new beta, never a patched promotion; someone who installed beta.1 is never offered a 2.5.0 promoted from beta.2, because the app compares versions and both say 2.5.0.
**Falsifier**: a promoted release needing a fix within 48 hours for something a day's use shows; a beta offered to an installed app or named on `/download`; promoted assets whose SHA-256 differ from the beta's; three stable releases in a row cut without a beta.
