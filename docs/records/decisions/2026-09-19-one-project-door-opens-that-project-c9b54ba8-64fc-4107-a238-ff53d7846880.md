## 2026-09-19 — With one project, the rail's Projects door opens that project

**Why**: `<project>/atlas` is the standard folder shape, so most folders hold exactly one project. Pressing Projects then drew a list of one row above roughly 750px of nothing, and asked for a second press to reach the only thing on it (measured 2026-09-19, 1920x1080). The screen was correct and unfinished: a stop that answers a question the folder has already answered.
**Prior**: 2026-09-06 "A folder says what it holds; the rail reads the files" stands and is extended from which doors are drawn to where one of them leads. 2026-09-15 "Projects stays a compact index for identification and selection" stands untouched: the list keeps its shape, and is what the door opens whenever identification is a real question (none, or two and more).
**Decision**: When the open folder holds exactly one project, the rail's Projects door and its keyboard shortcut open that project's page; with none or with several they open the list, as before. The list stays one press away on every project page, as the breadcrumb that already sits at its top left, and keeps being where a project is created.
**Dissent**: none recorded. The nearest objection is that a rail destination whose address depends on data is less predictable; the reply is that the rail already reads the folder to decide which doors exist at all, and that the second press was buying a choice between one option.
**Falsifier**: a person with one project who cannot find the list or "New project" and says so, or who reads the direct landing as having lost a screen. Then the door goes back to the list and the emptiness is answered inside it instead.
**Owner**: the owner (2026-09-19 instruction to decide and build); applied by Claude.
