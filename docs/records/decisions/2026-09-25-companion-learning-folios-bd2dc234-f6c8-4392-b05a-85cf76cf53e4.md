**Why**: The owner found the companion menus visually indistinct from the map and requested learning about the actual ontology and business, rather than activity rewards alone. A source-hidden walkthrough exposed a source-return detour and later silent truncation of a personal explanation.
**Prior**: The companion work-quests decision (2026-09-25, 8a0a7959-2bfa-4b0a-bb77-100285b30db5) stands: current evidence governs activity rewards, ordinary work opens progression, and rewards do not accept meaning.
**Decision**: Keep the selected persistent pixel world and in-game menus. Separate illustrated folios and framed HUD controls from the world. Add one camp learning path through a saved concept's responsibility and exclusions, its original direct dependency declaration, a review candidate or explicit unknown, and a bounded personal explanation. Reuse existing reading and reflection rewards. Read sources inside the game, preserve drafts on failed reads or writes, and recheck current saved evidence before completion. **Falsifier**: Reopen the decision if a source-hidden participant completes the path but cannot explain a cited responsibility and dependency boundary, if changed evidence can receive a completed reflection, or if a required action is hidden beneath another panel at a supported viewport.
