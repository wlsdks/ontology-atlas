## 2026-11-19 — The download page lets a visitor build a small map with Moru
**Why**: The owner asked for a hands-on part of the download page where a visitor builds an ontology together with Moru. The two films show the result; neither lets a first-time visitor do the one act Atlas asks of a person, which is naming what code does in people's words and judging the lines between those names.
**Prior**: Keeps 2026-11-17's two self-playing films and their order; the section sits between them, so the Markdown film's map and its file come first. Keeps the page's pause for its loops (WCAG 2.2.2) and its rule that facts never move. Keeps 2026-11-18's direction that concepts carry human names and that no approval step exists: the section offers no approve button, only naming, linking and reading a change.
**Decision**: A section of about one minute on a fixed sample repository (`shop-app`, eight files, three concepts, two relations, one commit): open the folder, pick a name for each group of files, draw the two relations Moru asks for, see an agent's commit land on the map, tap the concept it changed, and end on that concept's Markdown file. The copy claims only what Atlas does: the map marks the concept the commit changed; Atlas neither applies nor approves the commit. It is staged in the page, reads nothing from the visitor's disk and sends nothing anywhere. A line under it says it is a guided sample and that in real use the agent writes the concepts over MCP. It ends on the browser map (`/topology`) and the GitHub source, the source alone on a phone.
**Dissent**: A guided sample with two fixed name choices teaches a smaller act than the real one, where the agent proposes and the person corrects; a visitor may take Atlas for a drawing tool. It lengthens the page by one section on every device.
**Falsifier**: Visitors who reach the section do not finish it, or people who finish it still describe Atlas as a tool where they draw the map by hand. Then the section is shortened to the commit and its changed concept, or replaced by a film of the agent proposing names.
**Owner**: Project owner, 2026-11-19.
