## 2026-09-28 — A rendered definition preserves a complete authored introduction

**Why**: A source-hidden native walkthrough read an Excludes clause, then saw the same clause rendered as a positive definition in Meaning review. Generic excerpt flattening removed its boundary label.
**Prior**: Preserve the 2026-08-25 summary-freshness decision and the documented first-paragraph graph-summary contract; neither authorizes treating every preview block as a definition. Generic excerpts keep their list/table search contract.
**Decision**: A nonblank authored description keeps its full value. Otherwise project a complete opening prose paragraph, at most 320 characters, preceded only by blanks and a matching authored document-title H1. Stop at another block or section. Ineligible or overlong introductions show a truthful preview-unavailable state and retain the full-document action. Local and generated manifests carry a bounded independent field without retaining full bodies. Canonical meaning and MCP previews are unchanged.
**Dissent**: Inline labeled boundaries would expose more context, but duplicate the existing document and widen this repair. Conservative abstention removes some inline previews; the source opener remains.
**Falsifier**: An excluded or historical paragraph becomes a definition, a late qualifier is clipped, a missing preview is claimed to be a missing definition, or the field retains the full source body. Reopen if the intended full-document action cannot recover the context.
**Owner**: The owner requested design and functional QA with repair; one independent source-hidden walker identified the failure and the same reviewer approved the bounded spec after its retention proof was corrected.
