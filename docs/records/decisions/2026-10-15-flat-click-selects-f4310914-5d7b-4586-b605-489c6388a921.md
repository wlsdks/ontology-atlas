## 2026-10-15 — A flat-map click selects; zoom is the reader's gesture

**Why**: at 1920 a click on the meaning-layer domain zoomed the camera 0.890 → 1.601, folded the toolbar (1484 → 1092 px, pill moved 353 px, utility lane and fit tile gone) and INDEX; one Escape left the selection on and the camera 140 px off.
**Prior**: narrows 2026-10-02 "Flat ... shows detail by zoom" (`decisions/2026-10-02-flat-dial-88b537fc-3831-4e9d-823f-0465c90a65a8.md`): detail still arrives by zoom, never as a click's side effect. Overturns, on Flat only, the two-press Escape (popover, then deselect) from the 2026-07-21 M-7 fix (`e22484b5c`, the `M-7` cases in `topology-esc-ladder.test.ts`); no record decided it. Other views keep it, and keep INDEX folding for a selection.
**Decision**: the selection's source decides the camera. A canvas click keeps the scale and slides sideways on `SPRING.canvas` only when the panel would cover the node; double-click, Enter, search, INDEX, a panel link, a deep link and arrow keys frame it. Clicking the selected node keeps it. On Flat from 1024 px INDEX stays open; there and in Territories and Hex only chrome under the panel fades in place, while those two keep their two-press Escape. One Escape clears selection and chrome and returns the camera the selection moved from unless the reader panned or zoomed since.
**Dissent**: a click no longer frames a far node's ego graph; a reader who wanted it must double-click. Clicking a selected node no longer deselects.
**Falsifier**: the 2026-10-02 falsifier stands; also reopen on a click that changes the camera scale, an Escape that lands more than 1 px from an untouched pre-selection camera, or one that moves a view the reader panned by more than 4 px.
