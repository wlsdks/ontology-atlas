**Prior**: 2026-08-02 first-run card (lead as attention winner) stands; its separate agent sentence merges into the one body sentence.
**Decision**: one sample line named with the sample switch's own word and a quiet Close at its end, a two-line heading, one body sentence saying the map is drawn from the few lines at the top of each file, two 40px actions (open my markdown folder, the tour), the sample switch and size below a divider, then two closed disclosures: "More ways to start" (create a folder, the terminal command) and the domain, capability and element definitions with the plain-language switch.
