## 2026-09-27 — The question desk shares one document grid from entry to report

**Why**: The owner found the entry generic and the failed/no-match state misaligned. A large form gave no material to begin with; the separate activity banner displaced the question and named an unconfirmed file target.
**Prior**: The 2026-09-27 question desk and report/export decisions retain local search, explicit read-only investigation, citations, draft status, and guarded filing. Their authority stays unchanged.
**Decision**: Use a compact composer and up to three inventoried original-file rows. Entry, search, activity, and report share one outer edge. No-match owns its investigation action. Active, waiting, or failed work has a compact receipt and conversation recovery; completed reads reserve no banner. The owner delegated design judgment after requesting new references and image mocks. The selected mock uses only Atlas screenshots. shadcn/ui and Radix Themes principles were consulted under checked MIT licenses; implementation uses existing Atlas primitives, tokens, and motion.
