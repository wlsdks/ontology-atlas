## 2026-11-13 — On the Flat graph every selection frames like a click, and Escape leaves the camera where it is

**Why**: a concept chosen from the list, search, a panel link or a deep link used the focus zoom, which left a selected domain's name and count line under the detail panel (25 px at 1512x949) and zoomed an element until its lit lines ran off screen. A canvas click already used a gentler frame and already stayed put on Escape, so two sources of the same selection behaved differently.
**Prior**: overturns two sentences of `decisions/2026-10-15-flat-click-selects-f4310914-5d7b-4586-b605-489c6388a921.md` on the Flat graph: "search, INDEX, a panel link, a deep link ... frame it" with the focus zoom, and "one Escape ... returns the camera the selection moved from". It also records what 2516beba8 and b7180beff already changed for clicks. `decisions/2026-10-24-narrow-map-selection-index-18ea6278-c9d1-48ea-8661-cec45e28076a.md` stands.
**Decision**: on the Flat graph the selected mark, its member star, its name and its count line sit inside the free area between the panels for every selection source; a domain never zooms in; an element zooms in only while its far ends still fit, at most twice the zoom the reader chose. Escape clears the selection and chrome and leaves the camera; the fit control returns to the overview. Names that would land under a panel, the key or the tool row are not drawn, except the selection's own.
**Dissent**: a reader who jumped far through search loses the one-key way back to where they were (the 2026-10-15 reasoning); double-click, Enter and arrow keys still zoom, so two framings remain.
**Falsifier**: the selected name or count line is covered by a panel at any supported window size; or readers ask how to get back after a search jump.
**Owner**: stark
