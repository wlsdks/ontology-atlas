## 2026-09-13 — A held audit-log lock is waited out briefly, and one-at-a-time moves in process

**Why**: Release run 34723050267 refused a reservation on a healthy vault twice — `llm_audit.rs:526`, then `:779` on the re-run, both a `reserve` right after a `finalize`. `flock` belongs to the open file description, `fork` duplicates it, and `O_CLOEXEC` closes the descriptor at `exec`, not at `fork`, so a child of ours between the two owns the lock the parent just released; `llm.rs` spawns `curl` inside the reserved window on every send. A C probe of this module's exact sequence saw 0 spurious refusals in 20,000 rounds with nothing spawning, 2,894 of 4,000 with four spawning threads. For a person it is a refused LLM send on a healthy vault, blamed on a request that does not exist.
**Prior**: 2026-08-17 (52) stands — `O_NOFOLLOW` walk, link-count check, `flock` from reserve to finalize, tail re-verification, 0700/0600, fail-closed all kept. Its "a second request fails before sending" now reads "fails within 13 ms", and that promise's in-process half no longer rests on `flock`.
**Decision**: one reservation per audit log in this process moves to a registry of canonicalized paths, claimed before any file syscall and released by `Drop`, so a second reservation still fails closed at once; `flock` keeps only cross-process exclusion, and a busy answer that cannot be told from our own mid-spawn child is retried for a measured 13 ms (52 × 250 µs, covering the 12,658 µs worst hold seen in 80,000 probe sequences) and then still fails closed.
**Dissent**: the cleaner fix is to stop our own spawns inheriting the descriptor; not taken because it touches all 42 spawn sites and still misses a library's child. `fcntl(F_SETLK)` is not inherited but dies when the process closes any descriptor for the file, and the app reads this log.
**Falsifier**: an `audit-log-busy` on a healthy vault after this lands means the hold exceeded 13 ms, and the answer is the spawn-site fix, not a larger budget; raising the budget without a fresh measurement reopens this too.
