## 2026-11-14 — Architecture is drawn as a cross-section of the code: role bands holding real members, observed imports as arcs

**Why**: the ladder of role faces with sentences on every stroke, depth planes, a per-role ledger and an evidence dock read as explanation about the code rather than the code; a person could not see which modules a role holds or where imports actually run.
**Prior**: overturns `2026-11-12-architecture-sketch-one-chain-4b16b10e-50cf-4642-baa8-6b5ab3b1c502.md` and `2026-09-13-blueprint-ladder-takes-card-width-9ba7d7be-f7db-4364-9127-9d91bda6e518.md`, and in `docs/DECISIONS.md` 2026-09-03 "The ladder is chosen by height", 2026-08-30 "Every stroke says its sentence" (the sentence becomes each arc's accessible name and hover), 2026-08-30 per-role ledger and evidence dock, and 2026-09-08 depth planes; upholds 2026-08-27 "Role bands carry their occupants" and 2026-08-26 (a separate reviewed contract).
**Decision**: one row per role in profile order: mono path pattern and name, then a band of the role's real members as chips (`+N` overflow); right of the bands one arc per observed crossing between roles, bulging by span, 1–6px by count, neutral at rest, a violation dashed in the danger tone on the outer lane with `from → to N`; choosing a band brightens its arcs and shows their counts; nothing is drawn before a record; the header is title, one status phrase, rules toggle and agent action, and the last check's stamp moves into the rules panel.
**Dissent**: allowed-but-unobserved rules are no longer drawn, so the reviewed intent is read in the rules panel, not on the picture; empty bands for roles with no listed member carry only their pattern.
**Falsifier**: at 1280×800 or 1512×945 a role band out of view or a covered chip or label; a person who still cannot name a role's modules or the crossing against the rules from the picture alone; arcs unreadable as bundles on a profile of twelve roles.
