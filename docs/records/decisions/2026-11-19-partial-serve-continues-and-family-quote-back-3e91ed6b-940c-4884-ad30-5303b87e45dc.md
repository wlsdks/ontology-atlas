## 2026-11-19 — A partial serve gives the call for the rest; a write quotes back a family sentence read in part

**Why**: In the stage I grade, 3 of 7 declared gaps were functions served only in part; builders wrote the limit and never asked for the rest, and four of six builds followed no `deciding` note. Both false statements were universal sentences about a family, in a relation note or domain body, after one member was read.
**Prior**: Keeps `38369602` (family member count) and `5d4e1d65`. No floor returns: stage H's cost $4.62 more and had 6 false statements.
**Decision**: A read or context part cut from a longer declaration carries `continues` (it continues to line N; these lines are unread) and `nextCall`, the exact `analyze_repo_structure` line range for them (at most 200 lines, the file hash, the call's `rootPath` and `ignore`). A `deciding` note carries `decidingNextCall` until the caller's line was served. Where the outline may have missed the end, nothing is claimed. `add_concepts`, `patch_concept` and `add_relations` quote back, in `warnings`, a sentence starting every/each/all/both/only about a family tied structurally (a cited member or base, or a word naming the one base) when the session read fewer members than it has, with the count and the repair. Unknown families say nothing; nothing refuses.
**Dissent**: Handed-back calls may be ignored as `next` was; a warning after the write may go unrepaired; the word after "every" may mean something else.
**Falsifier**: In the stage J builds, no partial-serve `nextCall` is followed, or no quote-back leads to a rewrite.
