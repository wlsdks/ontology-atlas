## 2026-11-28 — The MCP server drops the proposal route and the compact brief

**Why**: measured 2026-11-28 on `docs/ontology`, `tools/list` was 65,710 bytes, and `analyze_repo_structure` still took `proposal`, `qualification` and `view` beside the breadth map, with a lifecycle, source digest and review schemas behind them. `agent_brief` kept a compact mode and `query_ontology` nine operations no question needs.
**Prior**: overturns the part of `0f78a22b` and `fbb466df` that left the proposal route and its candidates standing (and `e2b151fb` with it), and `38369602`: a symbol read carries no `family` line. Keeps `0f78a22b` (a first map in one run), `fbb466df` (breadth map, coverage) and 2026-08-17 (66). `disconnect_project_source` stays its own tool in both servers.
**Decision**: `analyze_repo_structure` takes `rootPath`, `ignore`, `sourceReads`, `sourceOnly`. `query_ontology` loses `all_paths`, `centrality`, `builder_context`, `match_edges`, `domain_profile`, `topological_order`, `meaning_repair_review`, `analysis_history`, and keeps `domain_matrix`, whose cross-domain counts are the app's Flat dial (an agent must be able to ask the same question); it also loses the arguments `iterations`, `nodeLimit`, `componentLimit`, `cycleLimit`, `recommendationLimit`, `orderLimit`, `minDegree`, `maxDegree`, `minInDegree`, `minOutDegree`, `slugContains`, `fromKind`, `toKind`, `relation`, `includeIsolated`, `dependencyTypes`, `componentTypes`, `seed`, `analysisMode`, `detail`, `task`; `agent_brief` loses `detail:"compact"` and `task`; the `competency` guide goes. Each answers `invalid_arguments` naming its replacement: the degree filters go to `sort:"degree"`, `slugContains` to `list_concepts({mentions})`, `relation` to `type`, `seed` to `slug`, the section limits to `limit`, `dependencyTypes` and `componentTypes` to `types`. A `null` retired argument counts as absent and `detail:"full"` is accepted and ignored, as the Rust server does. The review panel, qualification handoff, corpus evaluator and qualification contract are deleted. Next slices: smaller answers, batch `patch_concept`, cheaper fingerprints. The Rust port follows in its own slice.
**Dissent**: the proposal route gave a person a plan to review before any write. Not adopted: `0f78a22b` already moved review after the write, the route was the largest part of the manual, and the host's permission card still gates each write.
**Falsifier**: a real first build needs a candidate or plan the breadth map and `sourceReads` cannot give, or an agent holding a retired call cannot reach its replacement from the refusal.
