## 2026-09-14 — Section tabs use one flat selection boundary

**Why**: The owner rejected the raised tabs on MCP and Architecture, including their indigo top strokes, and requested a full visual redesign. NN/g's Tabs, Used Right supports a clear selection indicator, readable alternatives and proximity to the panel; W3C APG supplies the preserved keyboard and ARIA contract.
**Prior**: Overturns the raised shape in 2026-09-14 connected-document-tabs (ef7bf713-f24f-4762-ba7d-220b336039d9). Retains its seven-consumer scope, overflow disclosure, 44px coarse targets and enlarged-text containment.
**Decision**: Shared section tabs use a flat 40px minimum row, more space between targets, a 2px bottom indicator and stronger selected text. The top stroke, side framing, raised fill and document-tab corners are removed. Existing tokens and selection/keyboard semantics remain the authority; open-file tabs and mode switches keep their separate purposes.
**Dissent**: The raised shape can connect a document tab to its sheet. Here the owner observed it as an awkward box and line, and the seven panels do not share a matching sheet surface. The flat alternative must still make selection obvious.
**Falsifier**: The selected tab cannot be distinguished, a tab loses its linked panel or keyboard focus, coarse targets drop below 44px, or a selected label clips at enlarged text or narrow widths.
