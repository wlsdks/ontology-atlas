A force layout answers "what is near what" and needs a crowd; the Library's fact is direction: a source is read, a page written from it, a concept named by it.
**Prior**: overturns the picture in 2026-09-07 "The Library graph is a live force simulation with a bounded drift" (springs, gravity, drag-a-mark) and the rosette bars in `library-graph-picture.spec.ts`; keeps the 2026-09-08 stillness rule, the card, the hover dim, the 2026-09-13 stale amber contract, and the zoom ceiling at a 36px widest mark.
