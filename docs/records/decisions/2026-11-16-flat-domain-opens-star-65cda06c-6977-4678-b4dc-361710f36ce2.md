## 2026-11-16 — A pressed Flat domain opens its star across the free map

**Why**: The falsifier of `decisions/2026-11-16-flat-domain-select-zoom-a5cde444-6242-4b81-be37-c2de32faf1b7.md` fired the day it was written: the owner pressed a domain in the installed app and still could not press its members. That rule stopped at the least zoom putting 44 px between the two closest capabilities, so at 1410x840 a star filled 38 to 52 % of the free map with capability radii near 4 px and element radii near 2 to 3 px.
**Prior**: Overturns that record's "until its two closest member capabilities stand 44 px apart, at most twice the zoom it was made at"; its framing of the domain's own star, the deselect return and partners leaving the view stand.
**Decision**: A selected domain zooms until its star (disc, member capabilities, their elements) fills the free map, without the 2x ratio cap, stopping earlier only when its two closest capabilities stand `--map-graph-domain-apart-max-px` (160) apart. The star's marks open with the room they get: a mark and its nearest neighbour share `--map-graph-open-fill` of their distance in proportion to their largest open radii (capability 24 px, element 10 px), so few members draw large and many smaller. A selected capability's fanned elements open the same way. Measured on the dogfood folder at 1410x840 and 1280x800, dock closed and open: fill 68 to 93 %, capability radius at least 12.2 px, element at least 5.1 px, no overlap, no missed press.
**Dissent**: A domain press now changes the zoom two to five times, so more of the map leaves the view, and the move takes up to 720 ms; about a third of a star's members carry no name at the landing zoom.
**Falsifier**: Readers zoom out by hand right after pressing a domain; a member of an opened star is still missed; or a domain of fifty members opens into marks smaller than before.
