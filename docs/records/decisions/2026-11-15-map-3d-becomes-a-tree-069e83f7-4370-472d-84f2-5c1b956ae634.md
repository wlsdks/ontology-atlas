## 2026-11-15 — The 3D view becomes one tree; Strata and Neural are removed
**Why**: The flat map is the main view and stays. The owner rejected three 3D pictures in a day as the same map redrawn or as unreadable (kind planes, the flat map lifted by depth, a code-folder city), then chose the tree from three stills of the dogfood vault.
**Prior**: Overturns 090e33d4, ed02ff2e, e2d094d0, 6f3956bc and, in `docs/DECISIONS.md`, 2026-09-06 "Strata joins Cone and Cloud" with its legend rail, the 2026-09-07 tier legend and the 2026-09-07 line floor. Narrows fa1f007c (one 3D picker row), 2026-08-18 (76) (fog becomes depth tones), 645411dd (the slow-pointer turn clause), 0d8a6f67 (3D is a ghost travel, not a crossfade) and 4de4259c's falsifier (no Neural baseline). 2026-09-02's cone tree was dropped by 090e33d4; this tree has one trunk, no line at rest, and folds what does not fit.
**Decision**: 3D is one tree on its own canvas, outside the flat engine: project trunk, domain bough, capability branch end, element leaf; one parent per concept, siblings in id order. No relation line at rest; a pointed or selected concept lights its path and at most 64 dependency arcs with named ends. Stale is amber by the MCP server's rule. A frame draws at most 1,500 marks; denser branches fold into one counted mark and open nearest first under zoom. Drag turns, click selects, arrows walk the tree. Strata, Neural, the dome and their gates are deleted; `?view=strata`, `?view=coupling` and stored choices open 3D.
**Dissent**: Strata's by-kind reading and Neural's relation-driven grouping have no successor. A concept two domains hold is drawn once. Dependencies show one concept at a time.
**Falsifier**: From a five-second still of the dogfood vault at 1512 px a reader cannot name a capability's domain or point at the stale concepts; or adding one capability moves a concept in another domain; or an orbit frame exceeds 50 ms at 1,000 concepts. Then the layout or cut is corrected.
**Owner**: Project owner, 2026-11-15, chose the tree; delegated to the lead.
