## 2026-11-16 — The map corner keeps one door to the agent dock and no standing analysis entry

**Why**: The owner could not tell what the corner's "Analysis status" entry or "Meaning review" tile were for. Used by hand in the installed app: the entry shows no status and is a button renamed by the selection; with nothing selected it only asks for a scope; with one concept it ended at "cannot be checked or too large"; on the web it opens a card saying the check cannot run. The same check is on the concept's More menu and on a routed concept set. The Meaning review and agent tiles open one dock with the same tabs.
**Prior**: Overturns the "stable Analysis status entry without selection" of d4c00b25-124a-44fe-8159-91e36663ea3a; its inspector, explicit Inspect, Analyze next guard and saved-result association stay. Retains the one dock of 2026-09-05 "Meaning review keeps diagnostic analyses with their evidence and history" and the concept panel's entry of 86e81a90-93c5-482f-99d6-e1ab2dff6be8.
**Decision**: Remove the Analysis status entry and its single-project fallback scope: the check is offered only from a selected concept or a routed concept set. In the installed app the agent tile is the toolbar's only door to the dock; the web, which has no agent tile, keeps the Meaning review tile. A dismissed concept panel no longer keeps the corner tiles hidden.
**Dissent**: A returning owner loses a visible reminder that unknowns exist, and whole-project inspection from an empty selection is gone. The names "Meaning review" and the dock's tabs are no plainer; that stays open.
**Falsifier**: People cannot find the check on the concept menu, returning owners stop inspecting, or installed-app users cannot reach the dock's records and meaning tabs from the agent tile.
**Owner**: Claude, on the owner's 2026-11-16 request to rethink both controls from the user's seat; the owner's approval authority is unchanged.
