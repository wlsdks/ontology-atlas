## 2026-11-19 — The installed app keeps a derived index of the vault on disk

**Why**: owner, 2026-11-19: the app is the product and LLM wikis bring tens of thousands of files. Measured: the app's load costs 22.4 s CPU at 100,000 files; the app searched only bodies a person opened; SQLite's and tantivy's default tokenizers found 20 to 25 % of Korean matches.
**Prior**: fires the exit test of 2026-09-28 "Large wikis scale without a graph database" (43d4b4cc): app open 22.4 s against 10 s, MCP first call 10.5 s against 5 s. Keeps its "no graph database" and local-first's "no second canonical store"; rewords local-first's "Only the user's files and the browser's IndexedDB hold data".
**Decision**: `src-tauri/crates/vault-index` keeps per vault, in the OS cache directory, `search.sqlite` (FTS5, tokens only, one segmentation for indexing and queries: words whole, Hangul and CJK runs as overlapping pairs) and later `rows.sqlite` (per-producer cached results). A row is used only while its file's stamp matches; rows read within two seconds of an edit are compared by SHA-256. No frontmatter parsing, no user state; version mismatch or damage means discard and rebuild. Settings shows the size, deletes it and turns it off. Engine: SQLite via `rusqlite` `bundled`. Graph queries stay in memory; the web has no index.
**Dissent**: tantivy searched 100 times faster at half the disk, but both are under a keystroke and it refuses a second writer and adds 53 crates; a Rust core reached by MCP through a helper would fork the pure-JavaScript bundle; any vault-derived copy is a second place to protect, answered by tokens only, per-user permissions, Delete and an off switch.
**Falsifier**: the app shows a value the files lack after a stamp pass, or deleting the index changes an answer once rebuilt (withdraw it); ranked search over 50 ms p95 on the owner's largest vault, files over 1.5 × the vault's text, or a 50,000-file reopen over 3 s (reopen the engine).
