## 2026-09-27 — Library Wiki opens on a question desk with optional Jev advice

**Why**: The owner wants a searchable LLM Wiki that lets a person judge answers against original passages. A page list and selected-passage Ask do not give a cross-page question one inspectable place.
**Prior**: The raw-source / Wiki / ontology separation (2026-09-05), lazy local search (2026-09-11), and Agents Models Jev key, preview, receipt, and advisory boundary (2026-09-25) stand. This extends the last decision inside Library.
**Decision**: Keep `/library` and its tabs. The Wiki tab's unselected pane asks a question, reads bounded local Wiki claims and source units on Search, and shows anchors, coverage, omissions, and unknowns. Existing browse, Compile, and source reader remain reachable. ACP gets a no-write question brief; Ask disables automatic Wiki approval, while unexpected writes still require the existing permission review. No Jev key is needed for the core path. With a key, one selected claim and current cited passage may go through the fixed native Jev bridge only after a blocking popup shows the exact JSON, TypeSafe host, and authentication-key transfer and the person presses Send. Jev's answer is advice on that pair only. It changes no freshness, Wiki status, ontology acceptance, or write authority. Typing sends nothing; Jev does not re-rank search in this slice.
**Dissent**: A question-first pane may slow page browsing, and a quick Jev verdict may look like approval. Keep Browse explicit and source state separate; measure review burden and real Jev quality before broader automation.
**Falsifier**: A keyless user cannot reach an original citation; omitted files look like a complete search; a stale claim appears current; Jev sees text other than its preview or runs without a press; or its answer is mistaken for accepted meaning.
