**Observed**: five hygiene agents on 2026-09-27 (C5, C6, C7, C9, C10) ran `PLAYWRIGHT_STATIC=1 PLAYWRIGHT_BASE_URL=... pnpm checks:changed -- --run` in a fresh worktree; each failed at the e2e step with "Timed out waiting 120000ms from config.webServer" because no `out/` existed, then ran `pnpm build` and reran.
**Cost**: about 2 minutes of timeout plus a full rerun per agent, five times.
**Suspected cause**: playwright.config.ts starts `scripts/serve-static-export.mjs` for PLAYWRIGHT_STATIC without checking that the export exists, so the failure surfaces only as a web-server timeout.
**Proposed change**: script: playwright.config.ts refuses at load, outside CI, when PLAYWRIGHT_STATIC is set and `out/index.html` is missing, naming `pnpm build`.
