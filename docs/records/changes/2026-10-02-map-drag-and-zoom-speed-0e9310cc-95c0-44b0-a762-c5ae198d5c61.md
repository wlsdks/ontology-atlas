Settings, Map has Drag speed and Zoom speed: five steps from 0.5x to 2x, 1x by default, kept on this computer. Drag speed sets how far the map moves for each pixel you drag in every view; Zoom speed sets how far one wheel notch, one pinch or one + or - press zooms.
