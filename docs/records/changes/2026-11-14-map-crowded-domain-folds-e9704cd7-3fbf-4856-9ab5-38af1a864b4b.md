On a map with far more concepts than the screen has room for, each domain's capability discs now shrink together until none overlap, so a crowded domain reads as a tidy head of separate dots instead of a smeared cloud. When the window is too small for every domain's caption, the captions rest and the map draws larger; pointing at a domain brings its caption back.
