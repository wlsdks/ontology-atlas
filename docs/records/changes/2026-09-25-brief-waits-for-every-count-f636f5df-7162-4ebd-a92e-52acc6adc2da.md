The analysis brief no longer prints a partial count while the guidance scan or the code-evidence walk is still reading. Its headline says it is counting, behind the same two marks the list uses and with a light moving over the words, and the numbers and the list of what changed since arrive together once every part has reported. A later recount, such as coming back from another question, keeps the last numbers on screen and marks them as being recounted instead of changing them without a sign.
