Each Work status room now says what its worktree is doing. A room is one scene: Moru faces a screen on a console that lists the newest commits beyond the base branch, three at most, each with its age; under them how many files are changed and not committed, with the name of the one changed last, and the commit count. The bezel names the session and the state with how long it has worked. A new commit slides in at the top while the oldest fades out. Mini rooms show the newest subject with its age, and the floors below get a heading that says how many of their rooms are working.
