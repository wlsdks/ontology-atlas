Selecting a concept in the map's Neural view now lights everything a change to it reaches: every concept that depends on it, directly or through others, lit outward hop by hop as a signal spreads from the selection, in three strengths by distance; the plate counts the impact and the legend names indirect dependents. The map shows the whole reach, which equals MCP's impact (direction incoming) when the call's depth and limit cover it; the default call stops at depth 2 and 100 results.
