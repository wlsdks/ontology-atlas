On the Library's islands map, an island's name no longer stands on a neighbouring island's marks: it tries the seats under, over, right and left of its own island and takes the first clear one, may at most brush a neighbour's shore, and otherwise waits for the camera to close in. A name that fits across its island's body now sits inside it.
