The Library's Wiki tab now opens on an index of your wiki instead of two buttons and a stack of cards. At the top sit the search field and a + that makes a new page, then two rows: Log, with when the wiki last changed, and Check results, with how many findings wait. Below, pages gather in groups: Needs a look first, each with the reason it needs one, then Saved answers, your own folders, and pages from several sources, one source or none. Every row is the same height and shows the title, where the page came from and when it was written. Above 200 pages each group shows its five newest and Show all; Escape returns to where you were. Check wiki now sits at the top of Check results, and writing pages from sources stays on the Sources tab. If the page you are reading is deleted, Atlas says so once and closes it.
