Analysis Wiki and Guidance read in one order. Wiki leads with the card that has findings, counts each finding with its unit, groups advisory findings under their own label, folds empty cards into one line, offers one Library link per card, and ends on a line saying the list is complete. Guidance marks the role that reaches the fewest domains, its heading outranks the domain names, empty branches are drawn dashed, every stem is drawn once at one brightness, each domain says what it is for, the folder-wide collections sit above a rule, and text mode lines its counts up in columns. The no-folder examples keep the page title as the only headline and end on a filled next-step button.
