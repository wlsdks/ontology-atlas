The Library graph becomes a map of islands once a folder passes four hundred marks: an island per concept, its pages at the centre and the files they were written from around them, an Unsorted island for pages naming no concept and an Unread island for files no page has read. Every island carries its name and count; a stale page is an amber dot; no line is drawn at rest. Pressing an island opens it as the columns picture with a chip and Escape back to the map. On any folder whose page column folds, the folded pages stand in stacks, each with the files its pages read directly to its left, so no citation crosses another stack's names. Measured at 11,240 marks: 42 islands, every one named, under 5 ms a frame at rest.
