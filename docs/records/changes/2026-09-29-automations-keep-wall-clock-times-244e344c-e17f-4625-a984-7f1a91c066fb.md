Scheduled checks and reviews keep to this Mac's wall clock: on a daylight-saving night a five-minute schedule no longer goes an hour without a run, a two-hour schedule no longer drifts off its hours, and a 24-hour schedule no longer skips the day whose midnight does not exist; after the Mac moves to another time zone a daily 09:00 schedule runs at 09:00 there instead of at the old zone's hour, and that time is saved.
