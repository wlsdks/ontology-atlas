Reading a wiki page in the Library is now built around the page: links show the title of the page or concept they lead to, a citation opens the cited passage beside it with one line either side and a way to the source, citations that name their file sit on their own line under each fact, and the page ends with the sources it cites, the pages that link to it and the concepts it names. A result on the Wiki desk opens its page at that sentence, marked for a moment.
