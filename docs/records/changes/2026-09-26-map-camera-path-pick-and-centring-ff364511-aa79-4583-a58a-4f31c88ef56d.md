Choosing where a path starts (right-click, Path) now keeps every concept you could pick as its end on screen, framed between INDEX and the right-hand controls, instead of zooming in on the start and pushing its neighbours off the canvas. Territories and Neural rest in the middle of the free map like the other views, the flat overview stays centred at tablet widths with INDEX open, and the 3D views keep clear of the right-hand controls. Escape pressed while the settings gear has focus reaches the map again, as it does from the map's other controls.
