The map's 3D view is now a tree instead of the Strata planes and the Neural cloud: the project is the trunk, each domain a bough, each capability a branch end and each element a leaf, with stale concepts in amber. Dependencies are drawn only for the concept you point at or select, as arcs with their far ends named. Drag to turn the tree, scroll to zoom into a crown, click to select, and use the arrow keys to walk to a parent, child or sibling; a large ontology folds dense branches into one mark that opens as you zoom. Saved `?view=strata` and `?view=coupling` addresses open the tree. The flat map and the hex board are unchanged.
