A Library round's cadence is now a rail you drag (1, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or 1 to 24 hours) instead of four fixed chips, so a Slack incident room can be re-read every ten minutes while a release is out. A round also names the places it watches: folders inside this folder and one or more connected services, each narrowed to a channel, space or database, and the sheet reads the whole rule back as one sentence before you allow it.
