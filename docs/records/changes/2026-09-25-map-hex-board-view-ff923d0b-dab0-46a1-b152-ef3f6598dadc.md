The map has a new view, the Hex board, in the view picker after Territories. Every capability is one hexagonal tile, each domain a region of tiles around its title tile, the project at the centre. A tile's brightness and dots count its elements, a gold rim marks one whose cited code changed after its document, and a hatched dashed tile says the evidence is unknown (the web says so rather than guess). Tiles never move once placed: a new capability fills a free cell of its region. Strokes between regions count their dependencies and run only in the gaps between tiles. Hovering a tile shows a one-line summary and its routes; selecting opens the usual inspector. "Stale only" keeps the stale tiles and names the moved file, arrow keys walk to the neighbouring tile, and the wheel scales the tiles down to one nameplate per region. A link with `?view=hex` opens the view.
