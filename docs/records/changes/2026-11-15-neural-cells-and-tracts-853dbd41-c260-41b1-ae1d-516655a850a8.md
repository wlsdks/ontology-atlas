Neural now draws concepts as white cells grouped into one named cluster per domain, with each domain's counts above it. Only dependencies are drawn, as tapered lines; a stale concept is amber in the app. Selecting a concept dims the rest, lights what it depends on and what depends on it, and names both ends.
