On a narrow window the map no longer shrinks to a speck. The camera used to reserve a fixed 470 pixels for the side panel and the tool rail whatever the window size, which is more than a phone screen is wide; it now reserves at most half of the frame, and never less than what an open panel actually covers, so the graph uses the same share of the canvas on a phone or tablet as it does on a desktop.
