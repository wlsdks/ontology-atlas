The Flat map now moves to show cause and effect: a folder's first open assembles once in under 700 ms, pointing lights the reach as a wave from the pointed concept outward with each line drawing from its source, a selection then sends one light along each line from what is needed toward what needs it, switching between concepts goes straight from one picture to the next, and leaving retracts the lines faster than they arrived; a brief pass of the pointer lights nothing, and with reduced motion every state lands at once.
