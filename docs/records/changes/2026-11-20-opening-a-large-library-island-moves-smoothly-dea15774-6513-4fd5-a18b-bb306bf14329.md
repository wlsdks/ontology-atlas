Opening a Library island of tens of thousands of documents no longer stutters. Every frame of the opening placed every name again from scratch, so frames took up to a tenth of a second and names flickered while the marks travelled. Names now ride their marks while they move, a crowded name fades out instead of vanishing, and the names settle in their exact places once the motion ends.
