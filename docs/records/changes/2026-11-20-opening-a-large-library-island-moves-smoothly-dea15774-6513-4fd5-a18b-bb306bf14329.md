Opening a Library island of tens of thousands of documents no longer stutters. Every frame of the opening placed every name again from scratch, so frames took up to a tenth of a second and names flickered while the marks travelled. While the marks move, names already on screen move with them and a crowded name fades out; no new name appears mid-motion. Once the motion ends, names take their exact places and fade in over a few frames instead of all appearing at once.
