Library gains a Rounds tab in the installed app: a consistency round hashes every cited source and checks every page on a cadence with no agent turn, redrafting only stale sources when asked; a service round re-reads one attached connector once per pass and redrafts what changed. A round is approved once, in a sheet that states the exact scope and the daily agent-turn bill; a pass refuses anything outside sources/ and template-fitting wiki pages and names the refusal. The stage opens on a since-you-left card, then a time-axis ledger where held passes are quiet lines, changes are cards, and sleep is a hatched gap; a missed window catches up once.
