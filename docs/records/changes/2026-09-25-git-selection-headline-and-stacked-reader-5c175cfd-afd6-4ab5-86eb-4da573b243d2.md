The Git screen now gives the step or document you picked the largest title, named by the concepts it changed; the page title steps down to a label. The step shows a short id you can press to copy the full one, and a date in your own format instead of a timestamp. At the smallest app window the list and the document reader scroll as one page, so the reader is never squeezed to nothing. The discard button lines up with the end of the text it acts on, a document's other steps show three at a time, and neighbour names in the concept drawing no longer overlap.
