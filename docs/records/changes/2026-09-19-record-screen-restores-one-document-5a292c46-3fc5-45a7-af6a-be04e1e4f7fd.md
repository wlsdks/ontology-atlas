The Git screen restores one document: discard its uncommitted changes, or bring back the content it had at a past step. Each door states what is lost before it runs, nothing runs before the confirm, and a restore lands as an uncommitted change the person can read line by line and discard. A restore is refused when the document's identity at that point differs from the one on disk.
