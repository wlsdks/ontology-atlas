Play with Moru on the download page is now a one-minute arcade round: a 3-2-1 start, three lives, drops that come faster on a curve, a ×2 to ×4 combo multiplier, a rare golden star, bugs to dodge, magnet and slow-motion power-ups, and an end screen with the score, the best and a new-best celebration. The Sing, Hearts, Fish and Clap buttons are gone, and under reduced motion the round now plays calmly instead of turning off.
