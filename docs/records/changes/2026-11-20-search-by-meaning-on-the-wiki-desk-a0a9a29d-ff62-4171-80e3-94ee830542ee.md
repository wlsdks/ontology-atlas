In the app, the Library's Wiki desk can search by meaning: it finds passages that say the same thing in other words or in another language, and lists them with the word matches in one ranked list, each row saying why it is there. Turn it on per folder with a built-in model (downloaded only after you agree, about 2.3 GB) or a model you already run in Ollama; nothing from the folder leaves the computer. Preparation runs in the background with progress on every tab and can be stopped, and Settings lists the stored vectors and model with Delete.
