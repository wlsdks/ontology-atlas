The Screen settings pane fits its window again: the screen guide is one row with its on/off switch and its Show again button. The API Key pane says what connecting gives you once, as its opening sentence, and lists where the key is kept, what leaves and when without repeating them. The Update pane also says when the app last checked and which build you put off. In Workspace, importing nodes from another folder is a row of its own with a Choose folder button. On the first-run screen the companion's home is a card like the others, and the download page's release facts no longer run past the column on a tablet-width window.
