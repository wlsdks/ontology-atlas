The 3D map is now lit. Strata, the default 3D view, draws each level as a glowing translucent floor in its kind's colour, with a band for every domain on the floors below it, and Neural gathers its concepts around their domains. A concept's light is its evidence: it glows when its cited code is current, dims behind an amber ring when that code changed after its document, and stays dark behind a dashed ring when the app could not date it (always, on the web). A legend names the kinds and counts each state. A click selects without moving the view; a double-click or Enter flies to the concept and Esc or Home flies back. The Cone view is gone from the picker; if it was your choice, the map opens in Strata.
