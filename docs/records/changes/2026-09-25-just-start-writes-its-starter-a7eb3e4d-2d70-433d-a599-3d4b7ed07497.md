"Just start" and "Create a new folder" on the first-run screen now leave the starter you chose in the folder: the map's starter nodes, the wiki template and the agent guides are written before the folder is first shown, and "Just start" says where it made the folder. Both used to open an empty folder with no word at all, because the screen that pressed them was gone before it could write. If the starter cannot be written, a message says so and points to Settings › Workspace. The door also names the right place (Ontology Atlas in your home folder, not Documents), and the map's frame meter reads entirely in the screen's language.
