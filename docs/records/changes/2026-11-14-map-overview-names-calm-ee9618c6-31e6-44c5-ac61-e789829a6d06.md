The map's overview is calmer on a large folder. Names no longer print over one another or over the project's caption line: they are kept apart across the whole zoom step, and a name keeps its side as you zoom in. A domain whose capability dots have folded for crowding names only its two most connected capabilities until you point at it or zoom in, and the crowded group outside every domain is captioned by its count alone.
