The companion expedition now frames its original fox and creatures on a shared pixel combat floor. A phase-linked strike, impact, enemy windup, and dodge warning make the existing automatic turn and Space action readable. Camera bounds keep both actors visible in narrow viewports, movement stops at the foreground floor, and walk-frame changes no longer re-render the whole game world.
