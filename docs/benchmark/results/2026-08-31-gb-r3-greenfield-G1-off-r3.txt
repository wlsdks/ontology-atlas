# Northstar Commerce

Northstar helps independent merchants accept orders and keep sellable stock accurate.

## Purchase

Purchase owns turning a reviewed cart into a confirmed order.

Customers review a cart, authorize payment, and receive an order confirmation.

## Inventory

Inventory owns maintaining trustworthy sellable stock.

Merchants reconcile available stock across the storefront and warehouse.

## Installation

Run the workspace with pnpm.
