# Points Redemption

Lets a shopper spend points at checkout, alongside or instead of money. It leans on payment authorization for one reason: deducting points before a failed card approval looks, to the shopper, like points vanishing for nothing. Minimum spend, coupon stacking, and partial-cancel refund order are still open.
