# Customer Support

This area owns the question "how do we put a wrong thing right, and make sure the same thing does not happen twice?". The inquiry, the return, the exchange, and the judgement call about whether money goes back all live here.

The flow: the FAQ lets customers find the answer themselves first; when they cannot, an inquiry takes over. If the goods are the problem it splits into a return request or an exchange request, and when money is at stake the refund review decides whether it goes back.

Whoever arrives here has already been failed once. So the measure of success in this area is not "we answered fast" but "the same question never comes back".

Most inquiries are the aftermath of something that happened in another area, so the records here mostly point at orders, delivery and payment. The boundary follows that shape: this area listens and judges, while actually collecting the parcel is fulfillment's job and actually returning the money is payment's. The most frequent incident is those links going missing: when they are, an agent ends up with several screens open, asking the customer the same thing twice.
